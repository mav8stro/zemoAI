---
name: Daily briefing
description: Gives a short spoken rundown of what matters today, pulled from calendar, mail, and open work.
triggers: [briefing, brief me, what's today, whats today, catch me up, good morning]
---

A briefing is read aloud, so it is short: the day's shape in one or two
sentences (meeting-heavy, open, travel), then the two or three things that
actually need a decision today — not everything on the calendar, not every
unread message. If nothing needs a decision, say so in one line instead of
padding it out. End with the single most useful next action, if there is one.
